Ways to care

Adoption, Foster Care, and Volunteering

You do not need to do everything to make a difference. Start with one way to help.

How Adoption Works

  1. Learn about the animals and think about which pet may fit your home.
  2. Send an inquiry so the rescue team can learn about your needs.
  3. Talk with the team about supplies, care, and next steps.
  4. Follow the adoption process shared by the rescue team if it is a good match.

Pet availability and steps must be confirmed with the organization.

Our Foster Program

Foster families give animals a temporary home while a permanent home is found. They may help with meals, daily care, play, and updates for the rescue team.

Ask about the time, supplies, and training needed before you decide if fostering is right for you.

Volunteer Roles

Animal Care Helper

Help with safe play, cleaning, and other basic care tasks when scheduled.

Event Helper

Welcome visitors, share rescue information, and support community events.

Supply Helper

Sort donated items, prepare care kits, and help keep supplies organized.

Interested in a role? Tell us what you want to do and when you are available.
